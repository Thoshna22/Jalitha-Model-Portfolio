About

Jalitha Induwara is a Sri Lankan fashion model with a passion for fashion, fitness, lifestyle, and contemporary visual storytelling. After completing a six-month Professional Modeling & Personal Grooming course at Walk With Glamour Model Academy in 2026, he has developed a strong foundation in professional presentation, posing, runway presence, and camera confidence.

With an athletic presence and an adaptable approach to different creative concepts, Jalitha is interested in fashion editorials, commercial campaigns, runway, lifestyle, beauty, e-commerce, and brand collaborations. He brings discipline, confidence, versatility, and a willingness to continuously learn and evolve with every project.

Based in Mihintale, Sri Lanka, Jalitha aims to build a distinctive career in the modeling industry while working with photographers, designers, brands, agencies, and creative teams locally and internationally.
